## Result

Rejected as fix.

v10 improves direction and drift relative to baseline and keeps root height above v7, but it fails the reachability requirement:

- raw IK violation worsens to `2.979729`;
- planned-vs-FK and touchdown stay poor (`0.430222/0.731545`);
- swing accel is worse than v7/v8/v9.

## Conclusion

Soft loss stacking still cannot keep mixed-yaw direction, posture, and IK/FK reachability together. Current evidence suggests loss-only is near a structural limit for this failure mode.

The next credible direction is to change the planner target/output contract rather than continuing to stack scalar penalties:

- generate low-small foot targets in a reachable coordinate space;
- or optimize/score FK-realized foot positions directly;
- or export FK-realized touchdowns/feet after clamped IK while separately keeping viewer planned markers as diagnostics.
