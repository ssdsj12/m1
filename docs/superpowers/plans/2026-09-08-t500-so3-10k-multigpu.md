**Goal:** Repair the T500 right-palm SO(3) reference adapter, select one safe configuration with 10,000 total Isaac Sim steps on GPUs 4–7, and train a loadable offline latent model from the remaining teacher data.

**Architecture:** Pure CPU float64 SO(3) helpers produce geometric interpolation, spatial orientation error, and consistent angular feedforward without changing public arm-MPC dataclasses. A step-budget runner launches isolated Isaac Sim probes on currently free GPUs, records every consumed step, selects one candidate through fixed gates, and hands the remaining budget to teacher collection and offline training.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.10, PyTorch float64, Isaac Sim 4.5, Isaac Lab 2.2.1, Gymnasium, pytest, JSON/JSONL artifacts, CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES.
