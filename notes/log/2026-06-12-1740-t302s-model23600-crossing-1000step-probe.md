- Interpretation:
  - The policy walked stably and did not physically hit small obstacles in this 16-env training-scene sample.
  - However, it did not satisfy the proposed overpass definition: the only path-obstacle opportunity had root crossing but no foot-over event.
  - The negative clearance around the locked obstacle suggests the foot did not pass above the obstacle footprint according to the scanner-derived metric; likely the robot passed without a clean overpass, or the path/foot footprint metric needs visual validation.
  - Opportunity coverage was low (`1/16` envs), so this probe is enough to reject a success claim but not enough to estimate a stable success rate.
- Follow-up:
  - For a stronger first-layer test, use a controlled path-obstacle placement or denser path corridor eval so most envs produce opportunities.
  - Add visual/debug rows for the locked obstacle xy, root path, and foot trajectories before turning this inline metric into a formal eval tool.
